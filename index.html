F-1 Non-Lottery Immigration & Status FAQ

What is the F-1 Navigator and why bypass the H-1B lottery?
The annual H-1B lottery receives ~470,000+ registrations for only 85,000 visas—resulting in an ~80% rejection rate. F-1 Navigator models grounded, merit-based, treaty, and institutional pathways under Title 8 CFR (O-1A, EB-2 NIW, TN/E-3/H-1B1, and Cap-Exempt H-1B) that can be filed year-round without lottery dependency.
How does O-1A Extraordinary Ability work for STEM graduates?
O-1A requires meeting at least 3 of 8 regulatory criteria (scholarly papers, judging/peer review, critical employment role, high remuneration, or original scientific contributions). With 15-day USCIS Premium Processing, it allows rapid adjudication and unlimited renewals.
Can an F-1 or OPT holder self-petition for an EB-2 NIW Green Card?
Yes. Under the Matter of Dhanasar precedent framework, candidates with advanced degrees (Master's or Ph.D.) working in areas of substantial merit and national importance (such as AI, biotechnology, cybersecurity, or clean energy) can self-petition for permanent residency without an employer sponsor or PERM labor certification.
What bilateral treaty visas bypass the lottery for specific citizens?
Citizens of Canada & Mexico qualify for TN status (USMCA); Australians qualify for the E-3 specialty occupation visa; citizens of Chile & Singapore qualify for dedicated H-1B1 visas; and citizens of 80+ treaty nations qualify for the E-2 Treaty Investor visa to launch startups.
How are OPT unemployment days calculated?
Under 8 CFR § 214.2(f)(10)(ii)(E), standard 12-month OPT permits a maximum of 90 days of cumulative unemployment. A approved 24-month STEM extension adds 60 days, giving a cumulative limit of 150 days across the 36-month authorization.
How can I download and share my report?
You can download your personalized action plan directly as a high-resolution .PNG infographic or an editable Microsoft Word (.DOCX) brief for your immigration attorney, employer, or DSO. Reports are generated in real-time in your browser without email delivery.
